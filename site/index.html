Disclaimer

This is an unofficial, independent hobby project. It is not part of, endorsed by, or affiliated with the National Aerial Firefighting Centre (NAFC), any fire or emergency service, aircraft operator, or government agency.

Do not use it for official, operational, safety or emergency purposes. It is for personal interest only. For warnings and emergency information use your state fire service (for example NSW RFS, VicEmergency, QFD, SA CFS, DFES, Tas Fire Service, NT Emergency) or call 000.

Call signs, registrations, operators and bases come from the public NAFC call sign list and a personal spreadsheet. They may be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and bases shown are where an aircraft is believed to be based, not where it is now.

Aircraft positions come from volunteer ADS-B networks. They can be delayed, missing or inaccurate, and many firefighting aircraft do not transmit at all. Flights, trails and alerts are recorded automatically and can contain errors. Fire incident information comes from public state feeds and is shown as published.
